Lake Fictal Hostel

Rooms

Dormitories with 4 or 6 beds, two family rooms with private shower. Bed linen is included.

More

Breakfast is served from 7 to 9.30 am (CHF 12). Packed lunches for hikers can be ordered the evening before at reception.

Getting here

Bus 21 from the station to the stop "Lake", then 5 minutes on foot.

Lake path closed for repairs

The lakeside path between the pier and the campsite is closed until 20 November 2026.
