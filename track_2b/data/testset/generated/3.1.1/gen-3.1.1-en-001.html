Getting here

Lake Fictal Hostel is a ten-minute walk from Beispielwil railway station. Follow Lake Road towards the harbour and turn left after the boat landing stage.

By car: there are only six parking spaces at the hostel. Please book a space in advance; a day ticket costs CHF 12.

Reception is open daily from 7.30 am to 10 pm. If you arrive later, please call us on +41 32 000 88 99 and we will leave your key in the key safe.
